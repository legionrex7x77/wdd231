1. Site Name

Chalco Solar Lab

This name was chosen because it pairs the local setting of Chalco, Estado de México with a practical, experimentation-focused identity ("Lab") centered on designing, sizing, and evaluating off-grid solar power setups.

Representative Domain: chalcosolarlab.org

2. Site Purpose

Chalco Solar Lab provides practical calculation tools and structured hardware reference information to help users plan off-grid solar energy systems for homes, workshops, and remote loads:

  • Solar Load & Sizing Calculator: Estimates daily watt-hour (Wh) consumption, battery bank capacity (Ah/Wh), and solar array requirements from user inputs, saving recent configurations in localStorage and showing calculation details in a modal.
  • Component Reference Directory: Displays filterable equipment data from a JSON dataset comparing monocrystalline solar panels, charge controllers, inverters, and LiFePO4 battery options.
  • Field & Setup Guides: Summarizes core sizing concepts, autonomy days, and practical wiring/maintenance considerations.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

  • How many watt-hours per day and how much battery capacity do I need to run essential lights, router/networking gear, and small tools during power outages?
  • How do monocrystalline panels, MPPT charge controllers, and LiFePO4 battery banks compare for a small off-grid setup?
  • How can I save and revisit my previous solar system sizing estimates in the browser without creating an account?

4. Color Scheme

The palette pairs deep navy and slate-teal tones inspired by technical engineering and clear skies with warm amber accents evoking solar energy, all checked for high contrast against light surfaces:

Deep Navy (#0f2942): Header banner, primary section headings, and primary UI accents.
Slate Teal (#1e5f74): Navigation bar, link highlights, and card accent borders.
Solar Amber (#e09f1f): Highlight badges, focus indicators, and key callout accents.
Soft Cool Gray (#f4f6f8) & White (#ffffff): Page background (#f4f6f8) and elevated content cards (#ffffff).
Charcoal Ink (#18222f): Primary body text for high readability.

5. Typography

Two complementary Google Fonts are used across the site and applied on this site plan document:

  • Montserrat (sans-serif): Applied to h1, h2, and navigation/brand headings for clear visual hierarchy.
  • Inter (sans-serif): Applied to body, paragraphs, lists, and code/label text for clean legibility at both mobile and desktop sizes.
