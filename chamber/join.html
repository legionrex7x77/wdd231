NP Membership Benefits

Designed for registered non-profit organizations in Chalco at no membership fee.

  • Standard listing in the Chamber Business Directory
  • Access to monthly community announcements and newsletters
  • Invitations to public community networking mixers

Bronze Membership Benefits

Ideal for small businesses establishing a presence in the Chalco market.

  • Verified listing in the Chamber Business Directory
  • Discounted admission to workshops and training sessions
  • Quarterly feature in the Chamber member bulletin

Silver Membership Benefits

Built for growing businesses seeking stronger regional visibility.

  • All Bronze benefits plus priority directory placement
  • Eligibility for Silver homepage spotlight rotation
  • Social media spotlight posts and event discounts

Gold Membership Benefits

Our premier tier for high-visibility businesses and community leaders.

  • Priority eligibility for Gold homepage spotlight cards
  • VIP seating at business luncheons and annual expos
  • Featured promotional placements and training workshops
