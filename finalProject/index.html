Off-Grid Sizing Formulas

  • Daily Load (Wh): Sum of (Device Watts × Hours per Day) for each load.
  • Battery Capacity (Wh): (Daily Load × Autonomy Days) ÷ Depth of Discharge factor.
  • Solar Array (W): Daily Load ÷ (Peak Sun Hours × System Efficiency factor).
